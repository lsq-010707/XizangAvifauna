# Xizang Bird Checklist

# 西藏鸟类名录

## Introduction / 项目简介

**Xizang Bird Checklist** is a working project dedicated to compiling, verifying, and critically evaluating the bird records of Xizang. In addition to providing a checklist of species recorded from the region, the project places particular emphasis on tracing the **original sources of historical records**, evaluating the reliability of individual records, and identifying records that may have resulted from taxonomic, geographic, or historical misinterpretation.

**西藏鸟类名录**是一个用于整理、核实和考据西藏鸟类记录的工作项目。除建立西藏鸟类名录外，本项目尤其重视**历史记录原始出处的追溯**、单条记录可靠性的评估，以及识别可能源于分类、地理或历史地理概念变化而产生的错误记录。

The project is intended to be a continuously updated research resource rather than a static checklist. Species accounts will document the evidence underlying individual records, including published literature, specimen information, historical sources, photographs, maps, and relevant external resources where available.

本项目并非一次性完成的静态名录，而是一个持续更新的研究资料库。物种档案将尽可能记录各项分布记录所依据的证据，包括已发表文献、标本信息、历史资料、照片、地图以及其他相关的外部资料。

> **Current status / 当前状态：** Working draft / 工作草稿
> 
> This repository is currently private and contains ongoing research notes and materials that have not yet been prepared for public release.
> 
> 本项目目前为私人工作版本，其中包含尚在整理和核实中的研究笔记及资料，尚未准备公开发布。

## Language / 语言

The project will be **primarily written in Chinese**.

本项目将**以中文为主要工作语言**。

* `Checklist.md` will provide the scientific names in Latin and may include English common names where appropriate.

* Individual species accounts will be written primarily in Chinese and **will not routinely provide full English translations**.

* English will be retained where necessary in quotations, original locality names, historical source titles, and other bibliographic or documentary contexts.

* `Checklist.md` 将使用拉丁学名，并可根据需要列出英文常用名。

* 各物种的档案将主要使用中文撰写，**原则上不提供完整的英文版本**。

* 在直接引用原文、记录历史地名、文献题名以及其他需要保留原始表述的情况下，将保留英文或其他原文。

## Record status / 记录状态

The checklist uses a small number of categories to distinguish between the presence of a species in the checklist and the strength of the evidence supporting individual records.

本项目使用有限的记录状态类别，以区分“某物种是否列入名录”和“支持其分布记录的证据是否可靠”。

### Confirmed / 确认

A record supported by sufficiently reliable evidence from within Xizang, such as a verifiable specimen, well-documented observation, photograph, recording, or other credible primary evidence.

有充分可靠的西藏境内证据支持的记录，例如可核查的标本、记录完整的野外观察、照片、录音或其他可信的一手证据。

### Probable / 可能

A record for which the available evidence strongly supports occurrence in Xizang, but which lacks one or more elements required for full confirmation.

现有证据较强地支持该物种在西藏分布，但仍缺少达到“确认”标准所需的一项或多项关键证据。

### Doubtful / 存疑

A historical or contemporary record that has been included in previous sources but for which the original evidence is uncertain, incomplete, geographically ambiguous, or otherwise problematic.

历史或现今文献中已有记录，但其原始证据不明确、不完整、地理位置存在歧义，或存在其他足以影响记录可靠性的问题。

### Excluded / 排除

A record that has been critically reviewed and is considered insufficient or demonstrably erroneous for inclusion in the Xizang checklist.

经考据后认为证据不足，或可以确定为错误记录，因此不应列入西藏鸟类名录的记录。

### Unassessed / 未评估

A record or species that has not yet been sufficiently reviewed to assign one of the above statuses.

尚未完成充分考证，因此暂时无法赋予上述状态的记录或物种。

> **Note: / 注：**
> 
> These categories describe the current assessment of the evidence and may be revised as new evidence is discovered.
> 以上状态反映的是当前对证据的判断，并非不可改变的最终结论；随着新的原始资料被发现，记录状态可以进行修订。

## Project principles / 基本原则

The detailed criteria and methodological principles of the checklist will be added and refined during the development of the project.

本项目的详细编目原则、记录判定标准以及相关方法将在项目推进过程中进一步制定和完善。

1. **Geographic and temporal scope / 地理与时间范围**

   Due to limitations in personal expertise and available resources, the current review is restricted to bird records reported since the 20th century within the area currently under the actual control of the People's Republic of China.

   受个人能力和资料获取条件限制，目前本项目的考据范围仅限于20世纪以来记录于中华人民共和国当前实际控制区范围内的鸟类记录。

2. **Relationship with existing checklists / 与已有名录的关系**

   This checklist is developed based on the fourth edition of A Checklist on the Classification and Distribution of the Birds of China as the primary reference framework. It aims to further review, update, and evaluate individual records through additional literature searches and evidence assessment.

   本名录以《中国鸟类分类与分布名录（第四版）》作为基础框架，在其基础上进一步开展文献检索、原始出处追溯以及记录证据评估。

3. **Treatment of public records / 公开记录的处理**

   The publicly available records included in this checklist do not necessarily represent the earliest known records of a species in the region. Many early birding records cannot currently be verified through direct communication with the original observers and are therefore not treated as valid evidence at this stage. 

   Social media records are subject to unavoidable sampling bias. Most such records were identified through searches conducted after the existence of a record had already become known to the compiler. Therefore, these records may not represent a complete collection of available social media observations.

   本名录收录的公开记录不一定代表某物种在该区域的首笔记录。部分较早的观鸟记录由于目前无法联系原始记录者进行核实，因此暂不作为有效证据使用。

   社交媒体记录存在不可避免的收集偏差。此类记录大多是在本人已知该记录存在的情况下进一步检索获得，因此不可避免存在遗漏，不能认为已覆盖所有公开的社交媒体记录。

4. **Treatment of personal communications / 个人通信记录的处理**

   Personal communication records are included only when audio or visual evidence is available.

   所有个人通信记录均需具有影音证据支持。在缺乏公开记录的情况下，符合上述条件的个人通信记录也可能被纳入本名录。

5. **Treatment of publications and specimens / 文献及标本的处理**

   Records reported solely from research publications based on camera traps, bird surveys, or other monitoring approaches are not considered valid evidence if no photographic evidence or other verifiable evidence is provided.

   Records known only from museum database entries, unpublished information, or other secondary sources are not considered valid evidence unless the underlying specimen information, specimen images, or other verifiable evidence can be accessed.

   基于红外相机监测、鸟类调查或其他调查研究的论文记录，如未提供照片或其他可核查证据，则不作为有效证据。

   仅存在于博物馆数据库、未发表资料或其他二手来源中的记录，如无法获取对应的标本信息、标本影像或其他可核查证据，则不作为有效证据。

6. **Taxonomy and nomenclature / 分类与命名**

   This checklist follows the AviList 2025b taxonomy as the primary taxonomic framework.

   Chinese common names follow the Aboutbirds nomenclature system. Where discrepancies exist between current Chinese names and those used in the fourth edition of A Checklist on the Classification and Distribution of the Birds of China, the names used in Zheng (2023) will also be provided for reference.

   本名录采用 AviList 2025b 作为主要分类体系。

   中文名主要采用懂鸟中文名体系。当现行中文名与《中国鸟类分类与分布名录（第四版）》存在差异时，将同时注明郑四版中文名，以便参考。

## Repository structure / 项目结构

The repository is organized primarily around **individual species accounts**. Each species has its own folder containing the main account and associated evidence.

本项目以**单个物种的独立档案**为基本组织单位。每个物种拥有独立文件夹，其中保存该物种的文字档案及相关证据资料。

```text
Xizang-bird-checklist/
│
├── README.md
├── Checklist.md
│
├── Galliformes/
│   ├── Phasianidae/
│   │   ├── 360_环颈山鹧鸪_Arborophila_torqueola/
│   │   │   ├── Arborophila_torqueola.md
│   │   │   └── files/
│   │   └── ...
│   └── ...
│
├── Strigiformes/
│   └── Strigidae/
│       ├── 2809_领角鸮_Otus_lettia/
│       └── ...
│
└── ...
```

`Checklist.md` serves as the main index of the project and links to individual species accounts.

`Checklist.md` 作为整个项目的总目录，列出名录中的物种，并链接至各物种的独立档案。

Species folders may contain:

* the main species account (`.md`);
* relevant images and other files;
* other supporting materials.

物种文件夹可以包含：

* 物种文字档案（`.md`）；
* 相关图片及其他文件；
* 其他支持性资料。

The directory structure follows the taxonomic hierarchy at the order and family levels, while the species folder remains the fundamental unit of documentation.

目录结构原则上按照**目—科**的分类层级组织，而物种文件夹仍然是资料整理和考据的基本单位。

## Scope / 项目范围

The primary focus is on birds recorded, reported, or historically attributed to Xizang. Records of particular historical or biogeographical significance may also be documented when they are necessary to understand the origin or reliability of a Xizangan record.

本项目主要整理曾在西藏境内被记录、报道或历史上被归入西藏分布范围的鸟类。对于部分具有重要历史或生物地理意义、且有助于判断某一西藏记录来源或可靠性的区外记录，也可能一并进行考据。

The project will prioritize **evidence and traceability over simply reproducing existing checklists**. Inclusion in a previous checklist will therefore not, by itself, be treated as sufficient evidence for accepting a record.

本项目将**证据和可追溯性置于简单沿用既有名录之上**。一个记录曾被某份名录收录，并不自动构成接受该记录的充分依据。
