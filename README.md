# XizangAvifauna

**西藏鸟类**
**The Avifauna of Xizang**

---

## 项目简介 · About

**XizangAvifauna** 是一个持续更新的西藏鸟类编目项目，旨在系统整理西藏地区有记录的鸟类，并尽可能为每一个物种提供可追溯的记录证据和参考文献。

**XizangAvifauna** is a continuously updated project documenting the birds recorded in Xizang, with an emphasis on traceable evidence and references for each species.

---

## 项目结构 · Structure

The main checklist is available at:

**[`Checklist.md`](Checklist.md)**

名录按照 **目 → 科 → 物种** 的层级组织，每个物种条目包括：

## 记录状态 · Record Status

目前采用以下记录状态：

The following record statuses are currently used:

| 记录状态    | Status           | 含义 / Meaning                                                                                                                          |
| ------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **确认**  | **Confirmed**    | 有充分、可核实的证据支持该物种在西藏有记录。 / Sufficient and verifiable evidence supports the occurrence of the species in Xizang.                         |
| **可能**  | **Probable**     | 记录较为可信，但仍缺乏关键性证据。 / The record is considered credible but lacks key supporting evidence.                                              |
| **存疑**  | **Uncertain**    | 存在文献或历史记录，但其可靠性仍不足以确认。 / A literature or historical record exists, but its reliability remains insufficient for confirmation.         |
| **排除**  | **Rejected**     | 经核查认为该记录不应计入本名录。 / The record has been assessed and determined not to warrant inclusion in this checklist.                            |
| **未评估** | **Unassessed** | 尚未完成充分考证，因此暂时无法赋予上述状态。 / The record has not yet been sufficiently assessed and therefore cannot currently be assigned another status. |

其中，**排除（Rejected）**状态主要针对《中国鸟类分类与分布名录》第四版收录、但经本人考据后认为不应收录于本项目的物种。

The **Rejected** status is primarily applied to species included in *the fourth edition of A Checklist on the Classification and Distribution of the Birds of China* but determined, based on the present assessment, not to warrant inclusion in this checklist.

## 证据原则 · Evidence

本项目尽可能追溯到鸟类记录的原始来源。

This project attempts, wherever possible, to trace bird records back to their original sources.

对于**确认（Confirmed）**记录，本项目目前仅接受以下类型的证据：

For **Confirmed** records, the project currently accepts the following types of evidence:

* 公开发布在公民科学平台的照片、录音或视频；
  Photos, audio recordings, or videos publicly available on citizen-science platforms;
* 有公开照片，或经本人核实的标本；
  Specimens with publicly available photographs or specimens personally verified;
* 经过核实的物种新记录类论文；
  Verified papers documenting new species records;
* 附有照片、录音或视频的调查论文或研究论文；
  Survey or research papers accompanied by photographs, audio recordings, or videos;
* 采用分子生物学技术的分类学研究；
  Taxonomic studies using molecular methods;
* 公开发布在社交媒体，或经个人通信获取的照片、录音或视频。
  Photographs, audio recordings, or videos publicly available on social media or obtained through personal communication.

在极个别情况下，如果难以找到合适的公开证据来源，本人的野外观察也可作为可信记录。

In exceptional cases where suitable published or otherwise verifiable evidence cannot be located, the observer's own field observation may also be accepted as a credible record.

## 分类体系 · Taxonomy

本项目目前将 **AviList 2015b** 系统作为鸟类分类和学名的主要依据，并与*《中国鸟类分类与分布名录》第四版*进行对应。

The **AviList 2015b** taxonomy is currently used as the primary basis for bird classification and scientific names, with corresponding names from *the fourth edition of A Checklist on the Classification and Distribution of the Birds of China* also provided.

分类体系发生更新时，本项目将根据需要更新名录，并尽可能保留分类变更的信息。

The checklist will be updated when the underlying taxonomy is revised, with taxonomic changes documented where possible.

## 免责声明 · Disclaimer

本名录是一个持续更新的研究项目，并不保证当前版本已经涵盖所有符合要求的历史或现代鸟类记录。

This is a continuously updated research project and does not claim to include all qualifying historical or modern bird records.

对于历史资料，特别是早期探险和科学考察产生的记录，部分地点、分类名称和物种鉴定仍可能存在不确定性。

Historical records, particularly those originating from early expeditions and scientific surveys, may involve uncertainty in locality, taxonomy, or species identification.

因此，本项目中的记录状态代表的是**截至当前版本对现有证据的判断**，而不是对某个物种实际分布状态的绝对结论。

Record statuses in this project represent **the assessment of available evidence as of the current version**, rather than definitive conclusions about the actual distribution of any species.

随着新文献、新标本、新影像以及其他证据的发现，名录及相关记录状态可能发生变化。

The checklist and record statuses may change as new literature, specimens, photographs, recordings, and other evidence become available.

本项目的地理范围仅用于界定本名录的资料收集范围，不构成对相关地区政治地位或领土归属的判断。

The geographical scope of this project is defined solely for the purpose of delimiting the scope of this checklist and does not constitute a determination of the political status or territorial affiliation of any area..
